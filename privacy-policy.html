5. Retention and deletion

We keep your data only as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the App, meet legal duties, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. When it is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it.

How to delete your account and data

  • In the App: open the ⋮ menu → Help & Support → Delete account, enter your password, tick the confirmation box and tap “Permanently delete my account”.
  • By web request: visit delete-account.html or email dineshkumarjag238@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my account”.

We delete your account data within 30 days of a verified request. Some records may be kept longer where law requires (for example, transaction records), and backups are overwritten on a regular cycle.
