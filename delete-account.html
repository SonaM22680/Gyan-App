Delete your Gyan App account

Developer: Dinesh Kumar · App: Gyan App · Contact: shreesitaram9988@gmail.com

You can delete your Gyan App account and its data at any time. There are two ways to do it.

Option 1: Delete inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open Gyan App and log in.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu and choose Help & Support.
  3. Scroll to Delete account, enter your password and tick the confirmation box.
  4. Tap Permanently delete my account.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

Use this if you cannot open the app or forgot your password. Send the email from the address you signed up with, so we can confirm the account is yours. You can also ask us to remove messages you posted in chat or game rooms.

Email a deletion request

We delete the account within 30 days of a verified request and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your login account: name, email address and password (deleted immediately when you use the in-app button).
  • Your leaderboard entry: display name, avatar and score (deleted immediately when you use the in-app button).
  • Progress and settings saved in the app on your phone (cleared when you delete in the app).
  • Messages and photos you posted in chat or game rooms, and support messages you sent us: deleted within 30 days when you request it by email.

What may be kept

We keep a minimal record only where the law requires it or where it is needed to stop abuse, such as a ban or a report about a user. Backups are overwritten on a regular cycle. We do not use kept data for anything else.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account.
